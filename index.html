Contribution Guidelines

What You Can Contribute

  • Court judgments and legal precedents
  • Government policies and notifications
  • Research reports and case studies
  • Legal explainers and educational materials
  • Translations of existing resources

Submission Requirements

  • Documents should be in PDF or DOC format
  • Include complete citation information
  • Provide a brief summary (100-200 words)
  • Specify geographical focus and thematic area
  • Indicate copyright status and permissions

Review Process

All submissions are reviewed by our editorial team for accuracy, relevance, and completeness. We typically respond within 2-3 weeks. Approved contributions will be properly attributed in the archive.

Attribution

We acknowledge all contributors on the resource page and in our annual contributor acknowledgment. You may choose to remain anonymous if preferred.

Privacy & Terms

How we handle your information and the terms that govern your use of the Legal Portal.

Last updated: 1 August 2026

1. Privacy Policy

The Legal Portal ("the Portal") is a public-interest archive of laws, judgments, policies and research on India's commons. We are committed to protecting the privacy of the people who use and contribute to it.

1.1 Information we collect

  • Account details — for registered contributors: name, email, organisation and role, provided when an administrator invites you.
  • Contributions — documents, metadata, comments and any information you submit to the archive.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, searches run, and downloads, collected to improve the archive and produce anonymised analytics.

1.2 How we use your information

  • To operate the archive — hosting, indexing (including OCR) and full-text search of contributed material.
  • To review and attribute contributions, and to communicate about submissions you make.
  • To generate aggregate, non-identifying statistics about how the archive is used.

1.3 Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Documents you contribute may be made publicly available in the archive, with attribution unless you request anonymity. We disclose personal data only where required by law or with your consent.

1.4 Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw a contribution subject to the archive's editorial and legal obligations. Contact us at archive@legalcommons.in to exercise these rights.

1.5 Data retention & security

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, and apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. Published archival material may be retained indefinitely as part of the public record.

2. Terms of Use

By accessing or contributing to the Portal, you agree to these terms.

2.1 Acceptable use

You may read, search and download material for research, educational and non-commercial public-interest purposes. You must not misuse the Portal, attempt to disrupt it, or upload unlawful, infringing or malicious content.

2.2 Contributions & licensing

By submitting a document you confirm you have the right to share it and grant the Portal a non-exclusive licence to host, index and display it. Contributors remain responsible for the accuracy and legality of what they submit.

2.3 Accuracy & disclaimer

The archive is provided "as is" for information only and does not constitute legal advice. While we strive for accuracy, we make no warranty as to the completeness or currency of any document; always verify against the official source before relying on it.

2.4 Intellectual property

Government works and judgments are reproduced in the public interest. Other materials remain the property of their respective authors and organisations, credited on each resource page.

2.5 Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Portal and its partners are not liable for any loss arising from use of, or reliance on, material in the archive.

2.6 Governing law & changes

These terms are governed by the laws of India. We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version. Questions? Write to archive@legalcommons.in.
