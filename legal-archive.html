About the Archive

The Legal Archive for Commons brings together the laws, judgments, policies and research that shape India's shared natural resources — forests, water bodies, pastures and village commons — and the rights of the communities who depend on them. Much of this material is scattered across ministries, courts and gazettes; the archive gathers it into one searchable place.

Every resource is filed by type, tagged with rich metadata, and made full-text searchable through OCR — turning a pile of documents into a genuine research tool for practitioners, researchers, Gram Sabhas and civil-society organisations.

Contributors

This is a collaborative, public-interest resource built with partners working at the intersection of law, ecology and community rights across India.

About the Legal Lab for Commons

The Legal Lab for Commons is an initiative working to make commons law accessible and usable on the ground — translating dense acts and rules into plain-language knowledge, and tracking the judgments that are quietly reshaping how India's commons are governed and protected.

Contact + Contribute

We welcome contributions from researchers, legal practitioners, activists, and community members. Help us build a more comprehensive resource for everyone.

Contribution Guidelines

What You Can Contribute

  • Court judgments and legal precedents
  • Government policies and notifications
  • Research reports and case studies
  • Legal explainers and educational materials
  • Translations of existing resources

Submission Requirements

  • Documents should be in PDF or DOC format
  • Include complete citation information
  • Provide a brief summary (100-200 words)
  • Specify geographical focus and thematic area
  • Indicate copyright status and permissions

Review Process

All submissions are reviewed by our editorial team for accuracy, relevance, and completeness. We typically respond within 2-3 weeks. Approved contributions will be properly attributed in the archive.

Attribution

We acknowledge all contributors on the resource page and in our annual contributor acknowledgment. You may choose to remain anonymous if preferred.
